THE SHOEBOX & BINDER PROBLEM · 001

The binder remembers the cards. It doesn’t remember the details.

At a swap table, a Base Set Charizard is in front of you and another collector asks whether you already have one. You know you do. But which one?

Is your 004/102 Charizard the Standard print or the Holo? Near Mint, or the one with the soft corner and the crease by the flame? And is it in the binder, the deck box, or the tin under the bed?

At home it’s the same question in reverse. You open the binder to count your Pikachus and find three possible stacks: the trade tin, the pocket page you “definitely reorganised,” and a deck box with no label. The cards were never the problem. Keeping the details in your head was.

THE QUESTIONS IN YOUR HEAD

Which copy is mine?

  • 004/102 Charizard — Standard or Holo?
  • CONDITION Near Mint or Played?
  • 025/102 Pikachu — how many copies, and in which tin?
  • LOCATION Binder, deck box, or shoebox?
THE SYSTEM · 002

The catalogue knows the card. Your collection knows your copy.

Binderly keeps the reference shelf and your personal binder separate — so “Charizard” and “my Charizard” can finally mean different things.

Reference catalogueWhat exists

The card library.

A reference shelf of card definitions: the name, set, collector number, category, and the details printed on the card. It answers “What is this card?” — never “Do I own it?”

Name
The name printed on the card.
Set & number
The expansion and collector number that tell two similar prints apart.
Card facts
Category, Pokémon type, evolution stage, HP, and retreat cost when they apply.
BASE SET 004/102 FIRE STAGE 2

Read-only reference. Browsing the catalogue does not add a card to your collection.

Your collectionWhat you own

The copy ledger.

Every physical copy gets an entry. If two Charizards differ by finish or wear, they are two entries; if they are identical, the quantity goes up. It answers “Which copy is in my binder, and how many?”

Variant
The finish in your hand — Standard, Holo, Reverse Holo, or another listed print.
Condition
The physical state of that copy, from Mint down to Damaged.
Quantity
The number of identical copies behind one entry, not the size of the whole collection.
004/102 HOLO NEAR MINT QTY 1

Charizard — one physical copy, recorded as its own entry.

IN THE APP

Core tools

  • A separate entry for each physical copy.
  • Variant and condition recorded per copy.
  • Quantity that grows instead of duplicating.
  • Search by card name or collector number.
  • Sort and filter your own collection.
  • Card details when they apply.
  • Changes saved locally between sessions.
  • No account and no marketplace.

THE WORKFLOW · 003

Three moves from “I have it” to “here it is.”

No setup marathon. Look the card up, record the copy in your hand, and find it again without emptying every tin.

  1. Find in catalogue

    Search by name or collector number. The catalogue shows the card that exists — not yet what you own.

  2. Record variant, condition & quantity

    Match the physical copy: finish, wear, and how many identical copies sit in the binder.

  3. Search, sort & filter locally

    Retrieve the entry without opening every box. Everything is searched and sorted on your computer.

THE PHILOSOPHY · 004

Made for collectors, not resellers.

Binderly is a student project for people who like the collecting part: the hunt, the binder, the duplicate you forgot you had. It doesn’t buy, sell, trade, or price cards. It’s just a place to keep track of what you own.

FOR THE COLLECTOR

A place for the part you enjoy.

  • Organising the binder you already have.
  • Finding a card without emptying three tins.
  • Remembering variants, conditions, and quantities.
  • Keeping a private record on your own machine.
NOT A MARKETPLACE

No prices. No pressure.

  • No prices or valuations. Binderly never guesses what a card is worth.
  • No buying, selling, or trading. It is not a shop, auction, or trade board.
  • No accounts or social feeds. There is no profile to maintain and no audience to perform for.
  • No portfolio dashboard. The collection is not an investment to optimise.

The collection is the point. Everything else is admin.
