Collection statistics

Turn collection records into a clearer picture: how many entries you have, how many physical cards they represent, and how the collection is spread across expansions.

Open the sample dashboard How the numbers work

Feature status

Planned

Statistics are planned, not part of the current build; the first public release has not been published. Every number and chart on this page is an illustrative sample showing the intended presentation, not a live reading of the desktop application or your collection.

What statistics can tell you

Binderly’s statistics are about organisation and discovery, not financial value. They help you see the shape of what you own.

Ledger 01

01 · Scale

How big is the collection?

Separate collection entries from physical cards. An entry is one card, variant, and condition record; its quantity is the number of copies you own.

Two copies. Two cards. One entry.

02 · Concentration

Where are the cards concentrated?

See which expansions make up the largest share of the collection, and which sets are represented by only a card or two.

03 · Gaps

What might be missing?

A distribution can reveal gaps and imbalances — a set you thought was complete, or a type you collect far more than others.

In the ledger Counted

Organisation and discovery

Entries, physical copies, expansions, and the proposed type, condition, and variant views. These figures describe what is in the binder and how it is arranged.

Not in the ledger Excluded

Value and forecasts

No market prices, price estimates, or investment projections. A statistic can describe the shape of a collection without putting a price on it.

How the numbers are calculated

The intended statistics are built from the same collection records used everywhere else in the app. They are a read-only view — opening a chart never changes a card, quantity, condition, or variant.

Ledger 04

Entry count Rows

One record, one entry

Each distinct card, variant, and condition combination is one entry. Identical copies increase the quantity rather than creating another entry.

1 record = 1 entry

Physical card sum Quantities

Every copy is counted

The sum of the quantity field across all entries. One entry with a quantity of four contributes four cards.

quantity 4 = 4 physical cards

Reading the ledger

How each figure is built

Expansion distribution
Physical cards grouped by the expansion recorded for their card. A card that does not have an expansion value should be handled clearly rather than silently assigned to a set.
Additional distributions
Type, condition, and variant views would use the same records. They are not confirmed for the current application.
Not financial data
There are no market values, price estimates, or investment projections. Statistics describe organisation and collection shape only.

Local data When statistics are implemented, they should be calculated from the locally stored collection — no account, cloud service, or permanent internet connection required.
